# 3–5 minute demo script

1. **0:00–0:30 — Product overview**  
   Open the overview dashboard and explain the real employee, brand, coverage, conflict, leave, and holiday KPIs.

2. **0:30–1:00 — Organization data**  
   Open Employees and Brands & Processes. Explain the 36 employees, 30 brands, operational pods, 80% single-resource brands, and overlapping assignments.

3. **1:00–2:00 — Monthly generation**  
   Open Monthly Roster, select a month, and generate the roster. Show work/off/leave/holiday indicators and one of the five roster views.

4. **2:00–2:45 — Conflict handling**  
   Open Conflict Center. Use a leave or unavailable-date change to demonstrate a coverage gap, affected process, and resolution message.

5. **2:45–3:20 — Validation and publishing**  
   Revalidate the roster, explain the draft/validated/published states, and show why an unresolved conflict blocks publishing.

6. **3:20–3:50 — Existing scheduler**  
   Open Weekly Store Schedule and select a store to demonstrate that the original OR-Tools scheduler remains functional.

7. **3:50–4:30 — Architecture and assumptions**  
   Summarize React/FastAPI/MongoDB/OR-Tools architecture, key assumptions, security, and deployment options.
